# VisaLang SEO and AEO

Help a reader identify the page task. Do not stuff keywords.

## Titles and descriptions

- The visible `h1` can differ from the `<title>`. The title should name the route or task.
- English indexable titles, including the ` | VisaLang` suffix, stay at 60 characters or fewer.
- English indexable meta descriptions stay at 170 characters or fewer and must be unique.
- Put the developed topic in the title once: Germany A1 family reunion, or Germany B1 settlement and citizenship.
- Do not chain visa, residency, citizenship, study, and work into a title that the page does not serve.
- Descriptions say what to verify and who decides. They do not promise acceptance, a fee, a date, or an outcome.

## Headings and links

- One `h1`. Later sections use `h2`, then `h3`. Do not skip a level for a directory entry.
- Link the Germany A1 hub, the Germany B1 hub, and the matching comparison guide when a page introduces those exams.
- Keep family reunion and settlement or citizenship as separate destinations.
- Point Chinese navigation at a real Chinese page or a labelled English page. Do not call an English URL Chinese.
- Add `hreflang` only when the alternate page is the same task in the other language.

## Indexing boundaries

- Canonical URLs use `https://visalang.org` and a trailing slash.
- `noindex` stays on commercial placeholders and on guides that are not in primary discovery.
- Do not add a thin page to win a query. Deepen an existing Germany A1 or B1 page instead.
- Do not write that the site is ready for an advertising program. Metadata is not approval.
