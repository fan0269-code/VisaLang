## Review decision

The retained official sources support the narrow authority boundaries, the regulated status of the named general-nurse profession, the structure of one named skilled-worker route, and the identity of the telc nursing product. The page can record `sourceReviewStatus: reviewed` for these bounded facts because every retained high-risk claim now names its authority, current official URL, checked date, support limit, boundary and reader action. Evidence maturity does not promote content maturity or discovery.

## Deleted or prohibited inferences

- Deleted the unsupported statement that nursing recognition often requires `telc Deutsch B2` or `telc Deutsch B1·B2 Pflege`.
- Deleted the statement that work-permit and Blue Card routes may accept B1 or B2 depending on profession; it mixed immigration, professional and certificate-acceptance decisions.
- Deleted the DTZ/integration-course aside because it did not answer the page's retained work/nursing workflow.
- Deleted the broad claim that permanent settlement and naturalisation accept telc certificates. Those routes have distinct authorities and evidence rules and are outside this bounded page.
- Prohibit any implication that a telc product page, employer request or national overview determines local professional recognition, a visa/residence result or cross-region acceptance.
