# telc Deutsch B1/B2 format and preparation: official-source review

Review date: **2026-08-14**
Target page: `src/content/guides/telc-b1-b2-exam-format-and-preparation.md`
Review scope: the general `Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1` and `telc Deutsch B2` products only. School, business, nursing, medical, dual-level and other variants are outside this review because their format and rules may differ.

## Review decision

Retain and deepen the page as a bounded exam-format and preparation workflow. The two product pages and their official mock examinations support the current structure and assessment rules. The telc FAQ supports a current general results estimate and identifies the selected telc examination centre as the owner of dates, fees and local delivery. The page remains `starter-overview`, `noindex` and advertising-free; a completed source review does not establish content maturity, local availability or acceptance for any administrative purpose.

## Safe publishing boundary

- Keep the page limited to general telc Deutsch B1 and general telc Deutsch B2.
- State B1 and B2 facts separately where they differ; never merge them into a single assumed timetable or delivery format.
- Treat telc as the authority for product format, scoring and central evaluation; treat the selected telc examination centre as the authority for current local scheduling, fee, registration, accessibility, result communication and certificate handover.
- Do not infer visa, residence, citizenship, admission, professional-recognition or employer acceptance from a pass rule or product page.
- Present the telc FAQ's result interval only as a current general estimate, never as a guaranteed delivery date.
- Use official mock tasks for the baseline and original prompts for additional practice; do not reproduce confidential or remembered live-test content.

## Page disposition

**Retain and deepen; continue noindex.** The page owns a distinct preparation decision: turn the selected exact product into a product record, run an official baseline, diagnose by subtest and productive-skill criteria, and recheck local execution. It should not merge with the route-acceptance comparison or the fee/centre workflow. It remains `starter-overview`, explicitly `noindex` and advertising-free until a separate maturity review demonstrates enough user value and authorises discovery.
