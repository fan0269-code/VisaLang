# FAN-270 SEO audit and remediation

Date: 2026-08-20
Issue: FAN-270
Baseline: `f680c6234611606c0f308bbf386ee714b027385a`
Branch: `main`

## Scope

This maintenance window audited the 100-route Astro production build for search metadata, canonical/indexability consistency, social-card metadata, structured data, language alternates, sitemap membership, and internal links. It did not reopen or change visa, exam, fee, eligibility, acceptance, authority, or source-review claims.

## Audit findings

- All 100 generated routes already had exactly one H1, a unique title and description, an absolute VisaLang canonical, parseable JSON-LD, and the shared Organization schema.
- The build contained 56 indexable routes. Existing noindex guides, commercial placeholders, policies, and thin category routes remained excluded from the sitemap under the fail-closed discovery policy.
- All 10 English/Chinese hreflang pairs were reciprocal, and all generated internal links resolved.
- Seventeen indexable English routes rendered titles longer than 60 characters. Sixteen were guides whose visible article headlines also carry necessary decision-boundary context; the seventeenth was the Germany B1 route hub.
- Every route used the default SVG Open Graph image and omitted `twitter:image`, social-image alt text, dimensions, site name, and Open Graph locale metadata.
- The Timeline Planner description exceeded the 170-character launch budget. The Germany B1 hub description was also close to the limit and less direct than it could be.

## Changes

- Added an optional, schema-limited `seoTitle` field so a guide can use a concise search title without changing its visible H1, Article headline, breadcrumbs, related-guide labels, or decision-boundary copy.
- Added concise SEO titles to 16 indexable Germany A1/B1 guides and shortened the Germany B1 hub metadata title. All indexable English output titles now fit within 60 characters including the VisaLang suffix.
- Replaced the default social reference with a 1200×630 PNG rendition of the existing VisaLang artwork.
- Added site name, locale and alternate locale, image alt text, image dimensions, Twitter image, and Twitter image alt metadata in the shared layout.
- Added explicit `index,follow,max-image-preview:large` for indexable routes while preserving the exact existing `noindex,follow` contract for withheld routes.
- Tightened the Timeline Planner and Germany B1 hub descriptions without changing their functional or factual boundaries.
- Added a focused FAN-270 regression and two production launch checks covering snippet budgets and complete social/crawler metadata.
