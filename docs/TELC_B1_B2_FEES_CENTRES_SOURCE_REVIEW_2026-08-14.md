Review date: **2026-08-14**
Target page: `src/content/guides/telc-b1-b2-fees-and-test-centers.md`
Review scope: finding and comparing local offers for the general `Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1` and `telc Deutsch B2` products. Prices, dates, seats, candidate deadlines and local contract terms remain centre-, product-, sitting- and region-specific.

## Review decision

Retain and deepen the page as a bounded centre-discovery and written-quote comparison workflow. Do not merge it with exam-format preparation: this page owns the local provider and contract decision, while the format page owns the official product and preparation baseline. Continue `starter-overview`, explicit `noindex` and advertising-free status. Bounded source review does not establish content maturity or make any local offer current.

## Safe publishing boundary

- Keep the page limited to centre discovery and local quote comparison for the two named general products.
- Treat telc as exam owner and licensor, the finder as a discovery record, and the selected telc examination centre as the authority for the final local offer and contract.
- Never publish a fixed price, date, seat, candidate deadline, cancellation outcome, refund rule or average from one centre as a telc-wide, country-wide or region-wide fact.
- Do not invent reasons for price differences. Compare only the documented inclusions and terms of dated written quotes.
- Keep telc's centre administration deadline separate from the centre's candidate-facing deadline.
- Route certificate acceptance to the actual receiving authority; neither this page nor a centre listing can make that decision.

## Page disposition

**Retain and deepen; continue noindex.** The page owns a distinct local-execution task: verify the exact product, discover centres through telc, confirm the licensed venue, gather comparable written quotes and save the selected centre's current regional terms. It remains `starter-overview`, explicitly `noindex` and advertising-free pending a separate maturity and discovery decision.
