# telc vs Goethe for German visa routes: official-source review

Review date: **2026-08-14**
Target page: `src/content/guides/telc-vs-goethe-for-german-visa.md`
Review scope: first-party sources owned by the relevant German authority, Goethe-Institut, telc, or BAMF. This source package supports the current candidate content slice; it does not decide an individual visa, residence, citizenship, professional-recognition, or exam-booking outcome.

## Review decision

The page must not recommend Goethe or telc as a universal default. Both providers appear in the German Federal Foreign Office's current provider list for language certificates that can be recognised in visa procedures, but the reader's exact visa category, required level, certificate age or module conditions, and any exception remain matters for the competent German mission or receiving authority.

Only the exam owner can establish the identity and structure of an exam product. The selected official or authorised examination centre controls current availability, dates, fees, booking terms, and local certificate delivery. Product existence does not prove that a visa office, local immigration authority, professional-recognition authority, employer, university, or integration-course provider accepts that product for a particular purpose.

## Safe content boundary for the target page

The following decision logic is supportable:

> Goethe and telc are both potential providers of German-language certificates used in German administrative procedures, but neither is a universal default across visa, residence, citizenship, integration-course, or professional-recognition routes. First ask the authority deciding the reader's exact case to confirm the required level, complete exam name, module combination, document form, and certificate-date rule. Only then compare currently available sittings at official or authorised centres by location, format, fee, booking terms, and certificate delivery.

Safe product facts may be included only when tied to the relevant exam-owner page. For example, telc offers general and profession-specific German exams, while Goethe also currently offers a profession-specific nursing product, `Goethe-Test PRO Pflege`. Product existence must always remain separate from receiving-authority acceptance.

## Claims that must be removed or materially rewritten

- `Goethe is the default`.
- `Goethe-Zertifikat is historically the most common reference`.
- `Many embassies name Goethe A1 or B1 directly` unless replaced by one named mission, one route, one current source, and its boundary.
- `telc is frequently used` for integration, nursing recognition, or work permits; the reviewed sources do not establish comparative frequency.
- `telc offers profession-specific language exams ... that Goethe does not`; Goethe currently offers `Goethe-Test PRO Pflege`.
- Any universal claim that one brand is more accepted, cheaper, easier, more convenient, or faster.
- Any current fee, date, seat, result-delivery, certificate-delivery, refund, or rescheduling claim not sourced to the selected authorised centre and labelled with its location and check date.
