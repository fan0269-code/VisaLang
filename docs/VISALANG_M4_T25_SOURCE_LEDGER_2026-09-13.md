## Source selection and precedence

1. Goethe’s B1 product information at https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/prf/gzb1/inf.html leads to B1 administration terms (2025-09-01 edition). Sections 1.1–1.4 and 6 support the adult product timing and module scoring; §7 points to the general Exam Guidelines. The general 2025-09-01 guidelines §§14.7 and 15 independently support module certificates, later-combination boundaries and resits. Later consolidated certificates have additional venue/network/time conditions; the article never reduces them to a universal certificate-conversion promise.
2. telc’s exact canonical product page links a ZIP at https://www.telc.net/fileadmin/user_upload/mock_exams/Deutsch/telc_deutsch_b1.zip. The research tool cannot parse ZIP files and the attempted shell download stalled, so no ZIP content is asserted as read. The official shop page https://shop.telc.net/de_DE/mock-examination-1-zertifikat-deutsch-b1.html was opened, then its actual free-download link was followed to the exact 5061-B00-010301 adult B1 PDF recorded above. Its cover, product title, edition, format and score tables were read. The misleading 5060 search PDFs are scaled A2·B1 and were excluded; the 5001 youth mock was excluded.
3. The 2021 adult mock’s older next-calendar-year resit wording conflicts with the current rules linked from telc’s canonical footer via https://www.telc.net/sprachpruefungen/zertifikatspruefung/agb-pruefungsordnung/. The current English rules (valid 2025-04-15) §7.3 set 12 months from the first results sheet; Appendix 1 includes single-level B1 and excludes scaled two-level exams. Current regulations govern resits. The mock is used only for scoring/format, never the obsolete resit deadline. The comparison article explicitly explains that precedence.
