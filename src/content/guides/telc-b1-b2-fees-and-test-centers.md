## Confirm the exact product before comparing centres

This guide covers two general German products only:

- `Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1`; and
- `telc Deutsch B2`.

A listing that says only `telc B1`, `telc B2` or `telc German` is not an exact product record. Do not transfer a price, date or policy from a nursing, medical, school, business, dual-level or other variant.

Before searching, save the complete exam name and current telc product URL. If you need the certificate for a visa, residence procedure, university, employer or professional regulator, separately confirm that exact product with the body receiving the proof. telc and an examination centre cannot establish that external acceptance decision.

## Keep the three authority layers separate

| Layer | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| telc as exam owner and licensor | The product exists, current central exam rules, and which institutions are licensed or listed as examination centres | Your local total price, seat, candidate deadline, cancellation outcome or external acceptance |
| telc centre finder | A first-party route to centres currently returned for the chosen country and location | That a returned centre currently offers your exact product, date, delivery format, seat or fee |
| Selected telc examination centre | Its current local offer, candidate registration, final price, payment and contractual terms for a named sitting | Rules or availability at another centre, country, region, product or sitting |

telc's current Rules and Regulations say that a test taker registers with an examination centre. They also describe the centre as the test taker's primary business partner and the party that invoices the final price. This is why the selected centre, not a general telc page or another centre's advertisement, must confirm the local offer in writing.

## Build a centre record from the official finder

Open the current telc centre finder and search by country and postcode or city. For each candidate location, keep a centre record with:

1. the centre name and address shown by telc;
2. the telc finder URL and date checked;
3. the centre's own website and contact details reached from the listing;
4. the complete exam name and delivery format you asked about; and
5. the staff reply or booking page that confirms the local offer.

A centre-finder result does not prove that the centre currently offers your exact product, date, seat or fee. telc's FAQ warns that some centres do not offer the full telc programme and directs candidates to ask the examination centre about upcoming dates.

If a proposed provider or venue is not returned by your current search, do not rely on a logo, reseller page or old directory capture as proof. Ask telc or the proposed examination centre to confirm the current licensed arrangement and exact venue before sending documents or money. Preserve the country, region and venue in the record; do not assume one branch or partner status covers another location.

## Request a comparable written quote

telc's FAQ does not publish one candidate price. It states that each examination centre can set its own examination fees and that the centre must provide the fee.

Ask each selected telc examination centre for the same fields, for the same exact product and sitting:

| Quote field | What to record |
| --- | --- |
| Product and sitting | Complete exam name, delivery format, date, venue and whether a seat is currently offered |
| Candidate price | Total amount, currency, taxes if applicable, and what the quoted total includes |
| Payment | Payee, payment method, due date and when registration becomes binding |
| Candidate deadline | The centre's own registration deadline, not telc's internal centre-to-telc administration deadline |
| Changes | Cancellation, transfer, absence and refund terms, including the version or date of the policy |
| Repeat booking | Whether the quote is for the full exam or an eligible part, and what evidence the centre requires |
| Access arrangements | Contact path, candidate deadline, documents and any locally quoted charge |
| Results and certificate | How the centre will notify you and deliver the result sheet or certificate |

Do not infer why two totals differ. Compare what each written quote includes. A lower headline amount is not comparable if the product, sitting, services, taxes, delivery or change terms differ.

## Separate central rules from local and regional terms

telc's current central regulations govern matters such as registration with a licensed examination centre, examination conduct and which retake arrangements may exist for particular products. The selected centre controls its candidate-facing registration prerequisites, fee and other contractual conditions.

For cancellation, transfer, refund, absence, payment disputes and local consumer terms, read the selected centre's current policy for the country or region in which you book. Do not apply a German centre's terms to another country, one franchise location's terms to another branch, or a past sitting's terms to a new booking. If a local policy appears to conflict with current telc examination rules, pause payment and ask both the centre and telc which rule applies to the exact sitting.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing only a level label instead of the complete exam name.
- Treating a centre-finder entry as proof of a live seat or current fee.
- Copying one centre's price, date or refund policy into a country-wide rule.
- Using telc's centre administration deadline as the candidate's registration deadline.
- Paying a third party whose relationship to the listed centre and venue is unclear.
- Comparing headline prices without recording currency, inclusions and change terms.
- Assuming the exam provider or centre decides whether an external authority accepts the certificate.

## Next action

Create the exact-product record, shortlist centres through the current telc finder, and request the same written quote fields from each one. Select only after the chosen centre reconfirms the exact product, sitting, seat, total price and current terms; then save the confirmation with the date checked.
