## Start with CIPLE product verification

This page begins after the requirement decision. Use it when your saved nationality authority record gives you a reason to verify CIPLE as a possible language-evidence product.

CAPLE identifies CIPLE as a CEFR A2 exam product and describes the product and its components. That establishes what CIPLE is. It does not establish that CIPLE is required for every nationality profile, that it is the only possible evidence or that a particular applicant's document will be accepted.

## Separate acceptance from product facts

Keep these decisions with the authority that owns them:

| Decision | Final source to check |
|---|---|
| Which nationality profile applies | Justiça nationality guide and the responsible nationality authority |
| What language evidence that profile accepts | The current procedure document list or a response from the responsible nationality channel |
| What CIPLE is and how the product is structured | CAPLE |
| Which centre, registration conditions, session and fee apply now | CAPLE and the selected authorised exam centre |
| Whether the submitted evidence satisfies the individual file | The authority deciding the nationality application |

Do not use the CAPLE product page as a substitute for the receiving authority's document requirement. Likewise, do not ask the nationality authority to confirm a local exam-centre booking detail that belongs to CAPLE or the selected centre.

## Prepare a verification record before booking

Your record should contain:

1. The exact nationality profile and official procedure URL.
2. The language-evidence wording copied from that procedure or supplied by the responsible authority.
3. The reason CIPLE appears relevant to that wording.
4. The current CAPLE CIPLE page and the date you checked it.
5. The selected authorised centre and its current registration terms.
6. Any unresolved question about document form, alternative evidence or individual acceptance.

Keep unresolved items unresolved. A booking confirmation proves that a registration was made; it does not prove nationality eligibility or future acceptance of the certificate.

## Why the requirement page cannot replace this page

The [nationality-profile requirement page](/guides/portuguese-language-for-golden-visa-and-citizenship/) determines whether you should investigate CIPLE at all. This page performs the later **product-verification** task.

The two pages cannot replace each other: the requirement page does not maintain local CIPLE execution details, and this product page does not decide the applicable nationality profile.

## Common mistakes

- Reading a general statement about CIPLE and treating it as an individual nationality decision.
- Booking before saving the applicable authority wording.
- Treating a centre's availability as proof that the certificate will satisfy the receiving procedure.
- Assuming a fee, session, result timetable or registration condition stays fixed.
- Assuming that evidence not described on the CAPLE product page is automatically invalid for the nationality procedure.

## Next action

If you do not yet have an authority record, return to the nationality-profile requirement page and stop before booking.

If the record supports investigating CIPLE, open the current CAPLE product page, choose an authorised centre and verify its live registration terms directly. Save those product details separately from the authority record so an exam update cannot be mistaken for a change in nationality law or individual eligibility.
