## Start after the IRCC requirement check

Use this page only after your requirement record identifies one of the Express Entry programmes and confirms that both TEF Canada and TCF Canada remain candidate tests.

IRCC currently lists both products for the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program and Canadian Experience Class, but it maintains separate conversion information for each test. Raw scores are not interchangeable and cannot be compared as though they used one common scale.

This page does not review Canadian citizenship evidence or another immigration programme. Reopen the authority page for the exact procedure if your purpose is outside Express Entry.

## Keep four decisions separate

| Decision | Source that controls it |
|---|---|
| Which Express Entry programme applies | IRCC and the applicant's current Express Entry record |
| Which test and result IRCC accepts | IRCC's current accepted-test list, validity instruction and test-specific table |
| What each test product is | Le français des affaires for TEF Canada; France Éducation international for TCF Canada |
| Current centre, session and registration terms | The selected official centre, checked against the relevant test owner |

An exam owner does not calculate an invitation or permanent-residence outcome. IRCC does not maintain each centre's local fee, seat or cancellation terms.

## Compare the products without ranking them

| Product | Product fact to verify | Local check before booking |
|---|---|---|
| TEF Canada | The exact Canada product, its four compulsory abilities and the result document issued for that session | Official centre, current session, registration terms, identity rules and result process |
| TCF Canada | The exact Canada product, its four mandatory tests and the owner-controlled result document | Approved centre, current session, registration terms, identity rules and result process |

Do not claim that either product is universally easier, faster, more valuable or more accepted. Compare only the official product and centre facts that affect your real booking options.

## Build a test comparison record

For each test, record:

1. The Express Entry programme and date of the IRCC instruction.
2. The exact test name and the matching IRCC conversion table.
3. The official product-owner URL.
4. The selected official centre and current registration page.
5. The four abilities included in the exact product.
6. The local conditions still requiring confirmation.
7. Any result or profile question that must go back to IRCC.

Keep the two tests in separate rows. Never convert TEF Canada raw scores into TCF Canada raw scores or the reverse.

## When to stop the comparison

Stop before booking if the Express Entry programme is unresolved, if the test name does not match IRCC's current accepted product, or if the selected centre cannot confirm the exact Canada test and current terms.

If only one product can be verified for the centre and timing you can use, record why the other option was removed. That is a logistical decision, not proof that one test is generally better.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing TEF Canada and TCF Canada before selecting the Express Entry programme.
- Using one test owner's scale to interpret the other test's raw scores.
- Treating a preparation provider as an official test centre.
- Assuming the closest centre offers the exact Canada product in the needed session.
- Copying an old fee, date, seat or result estimate.
- Treating a score estimate as a guarantee of points, an invitation or permanent residence.

## Next action

Return to the requirement page if the IRCC programme record is incomplete. Otherwise, complete one comparison record for TEF Canada and one for TCF Canada, then remove any option whose exact product or official centre cannot be verified.

Before paying, send Express Entry acceptance, conversion or result-validity questions to IRCC and local execution questions to the selected official centre. This comparison is terminal; it does not create another content step.
