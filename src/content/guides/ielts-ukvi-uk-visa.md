## Who this applies to

Use this page for a **route-first requirement check** if you are still confirming whether your UK visa, settlement, or citizenship route asks for a Secure English Language Test. It is for readers who need to identify the deciding Home Office or GOV.UK requirement before choosing IELTS for UKVI, IELTS Life Skills, or another SELT provider.

This page is not the right starting point if you have already saved the route requirement and only need to compare booking options between approved providers. In that case, use the LanguageCert page as a provider-choice follow-up, then recheck the current GOV.UK list before paying.

## Start with the Home Office requirement

The Home Office or the relevant GOV.UK route page is the final decision authority for whether English evidence is required, which CEFR level is named, which skills are assessed, and whether an exemption or different evidence route may apply. IELTS does not decide an individual visa, settlement, or citizenship outcome.

Do not begin with a product page or a test-centre calendar. First record the exact route name, the language-proof instruction, the required skills, the CEFR level, any exemption notes, and the official page you used. Only then should you match that requirement to an approved IELTS product and approved test location.

## Match the route to the IELTS product

IELTS for UKVI, IELTS Life Skills, and ordinary IELTS are not interchangeable labels. The GOV.UK requirement and approved SELT list determine whether a route needs an approved SELT product, whether speaking and listening are enough, whether a broader skills test is named, and whether the test must be taken at an approved location.

Use this checklist before booking:

- Open the Home Office or GOV.UK instruction for the exact route and save the current wording.
- Confirm whether the route asks for SELT evidence, which CEFR level is named, and which skills are required.
- Match those requirements to the current approved IELTS UKVI product and approved location list.
- Check the IELTS exam product page for identity rules, booking terms, cancellation rules, and local execution details.
- Keep the authority requirement and the IELTS booking record together so you can recheck both before submission.

## Why this page does not replace the LanguageCert page

This IELTS page answers the requirement-stage question: what must the UK route accept before you choose a test. The LanguageCert page answers a later provider-choice and SELT product verification question: whether a LanguageCert product still matches the Home Office list and the product information you are about to book.

If the route requirement is still unclear, do not use a provider page instead of the Home Office source. If the route requirement is already clear and you are comparing approved providers, continue to the LanguageCert guide and check both providers against the same official requirement.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming that a test brand alone proves acceptance for your route.
- Booking ordinary IELTS when the route or approved list requires a UKVI-approved SELT product.
- Treating another applicant's route or certificate as proof for your own case.
- Checking the provider page but not the Home Office requirement that decides the evidence rule.
- Keeping no record of the route instruction and approved location used at booking time.

## Next action

Open the current Home Office or GOV.UK route requirement first. If the requirement points to an approved SELT and you want to compare another provider, continue to the LanguageCert SELT guide as a provider-choice check rather than as a replacement for this requirement check.
