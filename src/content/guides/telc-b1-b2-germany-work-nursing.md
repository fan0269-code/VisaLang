## Start with the decision, not a level label

`telc B1` or `telc B2` is not a complete answer to a Germany work or nursing question. First identify the exact profession, intended work location, job, immigration route and body receiving the language evidence. Different bodies control different decisions, and one body's confirmation cannot be reused as another body's decision.

This guide is a verification workflow. It does not determine professional recognition, permission to use a protected professional title, employment, a visa or residence permit, or acceptance of a certificate in an individual case.

## Four decision owners

| Decision owner | What it can confirm | What it does not decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competent professional recognition authority | Whether the profession is regulated for the intended work location, which recognition or professional-authorisation procedure applies, and which current language evidence that procedure requires | Whether an employer will hire you, whether immigration permission will be granted, or how a telc examination is delivered |
| Employer | Whether it offers the exact job and which additional workplace or recruitment language expectations it applies | Professional recognition, permission to practise, a visa or residence permit, or the rules of a telc product |
| German mission or foreigners authority | Which entry or residence route applies and which current documents the applicant must submit | Professional-equivalence or licensing decisions, the employer's hiring decision, or telc product rules |
| telc and selected telc examination centre | telc defines the named exam product; the selected centre confirms current local availability, registration and delivery | Whether the certificate satisfies a recognition authority, employer or immigration authority |

Do not collapse these rows into one approval. A job offer does not establish professional authorisation or immigration permission. A recognition notice does not establish that a visa will be issued. A telc catalogue page does not establish that a receiving body accepts that certificate.

## Nursing: locate the competent authority

Germany's official Recognition Finder identifies `General nurse (m/f) (Pflegefachperson)` as a regulated profession. The official recognition-procedure guidance says that the competent authority is determined by the profession and the intended work location.

Use the Recognition Finder with your actual profession and work location. Save the resulting procedure page, the named competent authority and its current instructions. Ask that authority to confirm:

1. the exact recognition or professional-authorisation procedure;
2. the exact language evidence and any profession-specific examination it requires;
3. whether the certificate you plan to submit is acceptable for that procedure; and
4. any location- and applicant-specific documents or next steps.

Do not copy a threshold, accepted certificate or specialist-language process from another city, federal state, profession or applicant. The national portal helps locate the decision owner; the named competent authority controls the actual case instruction.

## Keep employment and immigration separate

The German government's skilled-worker portal describes a specific job offer as one part of the work-visa route for qualified professionals and separately states that a licence to practise is needed for a regulated healthcare profession. Those are separate checks, not a promise that a particular telc certificate completes either one.

Ask the employer for the exact job title, duties, work location and its written language expectations. Then ask the responsible German mission abroad or, where applicable, the competent foreigners authority which immigration route and documents apply. An employer cannot issue a recognition decision or immigration permission; an immigration overview cannot decide whether an employer will hire you.

This page does not turn a CEFR level into a general work-permit, Blue Card, settlement or citizenship rule. Those routes can have different statutes, authorities and evidence rules. Start from the exact route instruction, not from the certificate you would prefer to book.

## Identify the exact telc product only after acceptance

telc lists general German B1, general German B2 and profession-specific products separately. Its `telc Deutsch B1·B2 Pflege` page describes a nursing examination with written and oral parts and nursing-workplace content.

That product description establishes product identity and exam-owner facts only. It cannot establish that your competent recognition authority, employer, German mission or foreigners authority accepts the certificate. Before booking, obtain written confirmation that names the complete product rather than only `telc`, `B1` or `B2`.

After the receiving body confirms the exact candidate proof, use the relevant telc product page and ask the selected telc examination centre to confirm current availability, delivery format, fee, registration, identity, accessibility, cancellation, result and certificate-handover terms. Do not transfer local terms between centres or products.

## Stop conditions

Stop before choosing or paying for an exam if any of these is missing:

- the profession and intended work location;
- the named competent recognition or professional regulator and its current instruction, when applicable;
- the employer's exact job and workplace expectations;
- the responsible German mission or foreigners authority and the exact immigration route;
- written confirmation naming the accepted language evidence; or
- the complete telc product name and selected centre's current local terms.

A generic search result, recruiter statement, forum report or certificate label is not a substitute for any missing decision owner.

## Next action

Create the four-row authority record. Start with the Recognition Finder if the profession may be regulated, then collect the employer and immigration instructions. Only after the receiving body names the accepted evidence should you compare the exact telc product and selected centre.
