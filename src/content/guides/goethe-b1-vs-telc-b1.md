## Who this is for

**Goethe-Zertifikat B1 and Zertifikat Deutsch / telc Deutsch B1 differ in how skills are grouped, assessed and repeated.** Goethe uses separately assessed modules; telc groups performance into a written and an oral examination. The sourced table below gives the exact current comparison. Choose by the document your receiving authority needs, the format you have practised and the selected centre’s confirmed terms.

This comparison covers the adult general-language products named above. It does not cover DTZ, telc B1 Schule, a scaled A2·B1 examination, B1+, or a vocational test. A centre advertising “telc B1” must identify the exact product before you use this comparison. Nor does an available B1 comparison configure a B1 immigration route in the A1 checklist tool.

## Key decisions

### Start with the certificate document you need

Save your receiving authority’s exact instruction, including its route and document format. If it says only “B1”, ask whether the exact product and your proposed certificate documents fit. What a product hands you and what the file needs are different questions: a passed Goethe module is not automatically the full evidence set, and an unsuccessful telc results sheet is not the pass certificate.

Use the [settlement route guide](/guides/goethe-b1-germany-settlement-work/) or [citizenship language-proof guide](/guides/germany-b1-citizenship-language-proof/) when the unresolved question concerns the procedure. Exam-owner rules explain what the product produces; the receiving authority decides what to accept.

### Compare the assessed units before choosing a resit strategy

A Goethe module and a telc written/oral examination part are different units. Read the resit row as well as the score row. Keep the A1 repeat rule with A1; read each product resit row for its own repeat unit.

For telc, the current examination regulations control the partial-credit procedure. The older official practice booklet remains useful for its scoring table, but its older resit deadline wording is superseded by those regulations. Confirm the applicable partial-credit record with the selected centre when registering. For Goethe, ask the centre which certificate document it can issue for your actual module history; later consolidation has conditions beyond merely holding passing results.

### Compare local offers on matching terms

A product’s exam duration does not tell you the arrival time, break schedule, oral appointment, result date or certificate-delivery date. Each shortlisted centre must give those instructions. Compare the same booking scope: adult product, all required parts or named modules, delivery format, participant category and currency. Use [Goethe B1 fees and booking](/guides/goethe-b1-fees-and-booking/) to capture a local offer.

### Scenario: one part needs more practice

You have practised both official samples and want to leave room for a possible resit. First identify the unit that would have to be repeated under each product’s rules. Then ask the centre whether that booking is available, which existing result document it needs and what it would cost. Finally, check whether the certificate documents you would eventually hold match your authority’s instruction.

### Original asset: four-party responsibility map

Use this map when two answers appear to disagree. Assign the question before contacting anyone; a clear reply from the wrong organisation still leaves the decision open.

- **Receiving authority:** “Does this exact certificate document satisfy the language-proof instruction for my application?” Save the route, document wording and reply date. A statement about one applicant or procedure does not answer a different file.
- **Goethe-Institut:** “Which Goethe product rule explains these modules, scores or certificate documents?” Save the relevant rule section.
- **telc:** “Which rule applies to this exact general-language B1 product and its written/oral results?” Save the product title and current regulation.
- **Selected centre:** “Can you offer this exact booking, and what local terms apply?” Save the centre’s own response.

For a conflict, write both source dates and the disputed phrase. Send the narrowly framed question to its responsible party. Keep the answer unresolved until that party explains which instruction applies; choose by the owner who explains it.

### Original asset: preparation-fit observation card

Use a separate card for each product after trying its matching official adult sample. Record: the task attempted; the precise point where you lost time or misunderstood an instruction; the answer or recording you retained; one practice action; and what you will observe on a fresh attempt. For example, “I began writing before identifying all requested points; next time I will mark the requested actions before drafting” describes something you can change.

Keep that observation separate from product facts. One successful sample does not establish a pass probability, and difficulty with one task does not make a provider generally harder. Compare whether your available preparation time can address the observed gap. Use the [B1 study plan](/guides/goethe-b1-study-plan/) for that allocation rather than inventing a score conversion between the products.

### Scenario: both products fit, but one centre’s offer is unclear

Your receiving authority has confirmed the document forms you intend to obtain, and both named products remain possible. One centre gives a clear booking scope; the other advertises only “B1 exam”. Ask the second centre for the exact product title, adult version and parts included before comparing its date or price. An earlier advertised appointment should remain an incomplete option until the centre answers those questions.

If only one offer becomes clear, your record can say “one locally confirmed option” without declaring it the best exam. You can either proceed through that centre’s verified terms or continue searching through official channels. The unresolved option should retain its blocker and contact owner, so a later response can be added without restarting the whole comparison.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Comparing brands without product names:** copy the exact adult title from the official source and local offer before using the table.
- **Treating a combined score as sufficient for telc:** read the independently required written and oral thresholds in the sourced row.
- **Assuming every Goethe certificate combination is the same document:** distinguish module documents from an overall certificate, then ask the centre and receiving authority their separate questions.
- **Using an old practice booklet for current resit deadlines:** follow the current regulations linked in the resit row and confirm the registration process.
- **Turning preparation experience into a ranking:** record the task you attempted and your specific next practice action instead of assigning a universal difficulty label.
