## Start with a nationality-profile requirement check

This page answers the question that comes before exam selection: **which Portuguese nationality profile applies, and what language evidence does that exact procedure ask you to document?**

The Justiça portal organises nationality routes by the applicant's situation and says the documents to submit vary with that situation. A former or current investment-residence route can explain how someone reached this stage, but it does not by itself establish the current nationality basis, the residence calculation, an exception or the accepted form of language evidence.

Use the nationality authority's current profile and document list as the decision record. Do not begin with a school, an exam centre or a generic article about Golden Visa holders.

## Build an authority record

Before choosing an exam, save a short authority record:

| Record | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Nationality profile | The exact profile selected in the Justiça nationality guide |
| Procedure page | The current official URL and the date you checked it |
| Document wording | The language-evidence wording shown for that profile, without paraphrasing it into a broader rule |
| Unresolved boundary | Any question about residence calculation, alternative evidence, an exception or document form |
| Responsible channel | The nationality authority, IRN channel or responsible Portuguese consular post you will ask about that unresolved point |

If the selected profile does not give you enough evidence to decide what language proof is accepted, keep the question unresolved. An exam provider cannot close that gap.

## Golden Visa is context, not a language rule

Do not convert "Golden Visa" into a universal nationality-language requirement. This page does not establish that every former holder follows the same nationality profile, has the same residence calculation, needs CIPLE or qualifies for an exception.

Record the route you actually used, then return to the current Justiça profile that matches the nationality application you are considering. If two profiles appear plausible, ask the responsible authority which document list controls before paying for an exam.

## Why the CIPLE page cannot replace this page

This is the **requirement page**. It determines whether the authority record gives you a reason to investigate CIPLE or another form of evidence.

The [CIPLE product-verification page](/guides/portuguese-ciple-a2-for-citizenship-and-residence/) starts later. It helps readers who already have procedure-level evidence that CIPLE may be relevant verify the exam owner, product and local booking steps. It cannot replace the nationality-profile requirement check because CAPLE does not decide nationality eligibility or an individual's evidence acceptance.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with a CIPLE booking page before selecting the nationality profile.
- Treating a past residence category as proof of present nationality eligibility.
- Assuming every nationality route uses the same language evidence or exception rules.
- Asking an exam centre to decide a nationality-document question.
- Copying a fee, session or centre from a third-party page instead of checking the current CAPLE or authorised-centre record later.

## Next action

Open the Justiça nationality guide, select the profile that matches your situation and save its current document list. Mark every language-evidence question as **confirmed by the authority**, **unresolved**, or **not shown for this profile**.

Only move to the CIPLE product-verification page when your authority record shows why CIPLE is a candidate. If it does not, stop before booking and ask the responsible nationality channel for the missing decision.
