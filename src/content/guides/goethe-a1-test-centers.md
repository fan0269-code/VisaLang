## Who this is for

Use this page once the authority-provided proof question has produced an exact exam product to investigate. It is for readers who need to distinguish an official Goethe route or telc examination centre from a course provider, copied directory, agent advertisement, or expired listing.

## Key decisions

### Verify the provider route first

Goethe says its German examinations can be taken at Goethe-Instituts and exam partners. Start on the official Goethe examinations route, then follow it to the selected local centre. For telc, use telc’s official finder. A selling page and a finder entry are both leads: only the centre’s reply shows that it administers this exact exam, and only your mission says whether that product fits your file.

### Match the full product name

For the adult Goethe route, check for **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1**, rather than a children’s/young-people’s exam, an A1 course, or sample training. For telc, identify the exact product the centre lists and return to the responsible mission if the product is not already confirmed.

### Keep local terms local

The selected centre owns its current calendar, seats, payment path, identity instruction, test-day directions, cancellation conditions, results process and certificate collection or delivery process. Ask the centre and save its answer. Take the fee, deadline, ID rule and result claim from this centre’s own current reply.

## Germany fee route

Open the [Goethe-Institut Germany 2026 fee page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/pre.html), locate the adult Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1 / SD1) row, and read the course-participant remark. Then continue to the selected official Goethe-Institut Germany booking route and confirm the category, any tax or checkout detail, the intended session and the final amount. The country price page does not prove every exam partner uses that checkout.

## Beijing fee route

Open the [Goethe-Institut Beijing schedule and registration page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/cn/zh/spr/prf/anm.html), match the adult A1/SD1 row, read the local learner-category definition, and follow the current form and registration-result process. Beijing evidence is not a China-wide price or category rule. A listed amount does not guarantee a seat; wait for the official registration and payment notice and recheck local terms before paying.

### Scenario: a nearby school offers an A1 package

The school advertises an A1 course with a “certificate.” Ask whether it administers the exact examination, whether its site is reached through the official provider route, and who owns the booking. If it is a course only, it may support preparation but does not solve your examination search. Keep the course decision separate from the certificate and mission-acceptance decisions.

### Scenario: a directory lists a centre but the centre page has no A1 date

The directory is a lead, not proof of a seat or scheduled product. Reach the centre through the official provider path, then use its published contact channel to ask whether it currently offers the exact exam and how it announces registration. Leave the date unknown until the centre confirms it.

### Scenario: the centre gives a different ID instruction from another centre

Follow the selected centre’s current written instruction and clarify ambiguity directly with it. Identity check-in is a local execution rule. Ask the centre which exact identification it currently requires for the booking.

## Centre-contact script

> I am considering [exact product] at your centre. Please confirm whether you currently administer that exam and provide the official page for registration, current test-day identification, result/certificate process, and any cancellation or rescheduling terms. I will confirm visa acceptance with the responsible German mission.

This asks for operational facts without inviting the centre to decide an immigration outcome.

## Booking evidence pack

Keep the provider-finder link, centre page, dated screenshots or PDFs, contact reply, booking confirmation, and payment record together. Add the exact name entered in booking and the identity document you plan to use.

## Booking failure recovery

### Scenario: a name mismatch is found before payment

Pause the transaction. Compare the booking spelling with the identification that this selected centre says it will check, then ask through the centre's published channel how to correct the record. A rule from another centre is not evidence for this booking. Continue only when the current centre has supplied an observable correction or instruction that you can save.

### Scenario: payment already sent before the mismatch is found

Keep the confirmation and payment evidence private and contact the selected centre through the same verified official route. Ask what correction, cancellation, transfer or other process applies to this booking; do not create a duplicate booking, dispute the transaction, travel to the exam, or claim a refund outcome on the basis of this guide. Mark the issue unresolved until the centre answers, and recalculate any affected test, result or certificate plan.

### Scenario: the booking confirmation names a different product or session

Read the confirmation as the booking record; treat the payment receipt as a receipt. Send the centre the exact product and session shown in its confirmation, ask it to identify the current resolution route, and preserve both the original offer and the confirmation. If the product differs from what the receiving authority confirmed, return the acceptance question to that authority after the centre has clarified what was actually booked.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Booking a class instead of an examination.
- Assuming a centre listed for one product offers every A1 version.
- Calling an old directory an official provider confirmation.
- Transferring fee, seat, date, ID or certificate rules from another centre.
- Booking a visa appointment around an unconfirmed result process.
- Ignoring a spelling mismatch until test day.

## Four-owner responsibility map

For centre verification, four owners answer different questions. **National/orientation authority:** the Foreign Office can direct a visa reader to the competent mission but cannot authorise a centre. **Competent mission or receiving authority:** decides whether an exact certificate is suitable for the file. **Exam owner:** Goethe or telc publishes its official route to centres and identifies products. **Selected centre and reader:** the selected centre alone explains its currently offered product, booking channel, identity instruction and administrative process; the reader keeps dated evidence and reopens verification after a location, product, spelling, or booking-channel change.

## A verification pass before money changes hands

Run one final pass through the evidence pack before entering payment details. Can you open the official provider route again? Does the selected centre itself display or confirm the exact product? Have you recorded the date on which you checked the page? Is the booking channel the centre’s official channel rather than an unverified intermediary? Can the centre explain its current identification and administrative process? Any “no” is a reason to pause and ask one bounded question.

This pass is deliberately smaller than a legal or academic audit. It checks only that the local operational claim is attached to its owner. When a centre’s answer conflicts with an old screenshot, prefer the centre’s current written instruction and preserve both records with dates. When the centre’s answer conflicts with the mission’s certificate instruction, do not ask either source to rule on the other’s domain: clarify product acceptance with the mission and execution with the centre.

Recheck after a material change such as a different location, product, booking channel, applicant spelling, or test date.

If you discover that an official provider page no longer leads to the centre, reopen the provider route and ask the provider or centre through a published official contact channel. Record the change as “centre verification reopened” rather than as evidence that a booking has been cancelled or an authorisation has ended. Only the owner can state its current status. Likewise, an email promising a course place should be filed as course information unless it expressly identifies the exact examination and the centre’s official booking path. This limited record protects readers from accidentally treating related services as the same transaction.

For a group booking or an agent-assisted booking, ask who will receive the official confirmation and where the candidate can inspect the centre’s current instruction directly. Retain control of your own evidence. A third party’s summary may be practical help, but it cannot replace the provider route, centre terms, or the competent mission’s acceptance instruction.

Write down the centre’s answer verbatim where it controls a local step, including the date it was received. This avoids converting a conditional explanation into a guaranteed booking, result, certificate or identity outcome.
