## Who this page is for

Use it when the [study plan](/guides/goethe-b1-study-plan/) shows a missing prompt point or an unclear reader. The competent authority decides proof acceptance. This revision is not an eligibility result. The B1 rules say the writing module has parts 1–3, lasts 60 minutes, and is graded against criteria supplied to examiners, with a maximum of 100 points and a pass at 60. The adult training examples that group evaluation into task fulfilment, coherence, vocabulary, and structures were not reopened for this correction. This page does not claim those four labels were re-read, and it does not estimate an examiner’s score.

## Original error and revision

Prompt: write to a course office, say you will miss Monday, give one reason, and ask for the homework.

First draft: “Hallo, ich komme nicht. Danke.” Risk, not a guaranteed lost mark: the day, the reason, and the request are absent, so a reader cannot see the task. Revision: “Guten Tag, am Montag kann ich nicht kommen, weil ich einen Arzttermin habe. Bitte schicken Sie mir die Hausaufgabe.” Reason for the change: the reader, the day, the reason, and the request are now each present. That is a self-check against the prompt you wrote. It is not an official mark.

## Common mistakes

- Writing “this loses points” as if the unread criterion sheet had been applied.
- Treating a formal or informal opening as a universal rule the opened PDF did not state for your task.
