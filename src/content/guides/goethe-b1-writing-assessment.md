## Who this is for

Use an evidence-based self-review before requesting feedback on a B1 practice response. This page provides a working method, not an individual exam or application decision.

## Key decisions

Choose the exact product or application task first. Keep official requirements separate from the original practice or checking activities below.

## Keep the prompt with the draft

Write down the audience, purpose and requested content before you start an original practice text. After drafting, point to the sentence addressing each requested item. Leave an explicit gap where something is missing instead of giving yourself credit for an intended answer.

## Review communication first

Ask whether a reader can identify why you are writing and what response or action you want. Then check sequence, connections and register. Fix a paragraph that answers the wrong question before polishing isolated grammar.

## Use feedback as evidence

Ask a teacher or practice partner to identify one unclear passage and explain what they understood. Compare that reading with your intended meaning. Keep your self-review, their feedback and your revision separate; none is an official examination mark.

## Repeat with different content

Choose a new fictional situation and apply the same review steps without copying the old text. Consult the official training material for its assessment references. Do not invent point deductions, a universal word-count rule or a pass threshold from this editorial checklist.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not treat a sample, practice result or another person’s experience as confirmation of your own booking, certificate acceptance or readiness. Record unresolved questions rather than filling in dates, fees or outcomes.
