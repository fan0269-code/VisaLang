## Who this is for

**The fee you need is the selected centre’s current offer for your exact adult Goethe-Zertifikat B1 booking.** Confirm whether it covers all required modules or named modules, which participant category applies, and what payment and cancellation terms govern it. The page supplies a quote-comparison worksheet only: a universal B1 price, a reserved place, an eligibility finding and a proof acceptance each belong to a different owner.

Use it after checking which certificate documents your receiving authority needs. An exam payment, module booking or available seat does not establish immigration eligibility or proof acceptance. Goethe controls the product rules, your selected official or authorised centre controls its transaction, and the receiving authority controls acceptance.

## Key decisions

### Identify the booking scope before copying a fee

Record the exact product as **Goethe-Zertifikat B1, adult version**. Then record the modules you intend to book. If you already hold results, give the centre their actual documents and ask what further booking and certificate output would apply. The [B1 comparison](/guides/goethe-b1-vs-telc-b1/) explains the product’s module and certificate rules.

A price for one module, a full booking or a particular participant category cannot answer a different quote. Call a module total a module total; a full-exam offer needs the centre's own confirmation. Retake charges and certificate services also require their own current terms.

### Reach the centre through the official channel

For Germany, start with [Goethe’s testing-location directory](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/ort.html), follow the relevant B1 location link and identify the institution that will take the booking. The [Germany exam-fee page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/pre.html) can support only its stated Germany scope and category. It does not set prices for another country or every partner centre.

Outside Germany, start with the [Goethe examination directory](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/prf.html) and the official site for your actual location. A search advertisement, copied screenshot or centre-finder entry alone is not a current booking offer. If the local page and a written reply disagree, ask that centre to resolve the conflict before paying.

### Keep exam timing separate from document timing

Ask for the actual examination-day instructions, including arrival and any separately scheduled module or oral appointment. Separately ask how results are communicated and how the certificate document is provided. The duration of an exam task does not determine a result-release or delivery date.

If your application target is close, take the centre’s stated process back to the receiving authority and ask what its submission instruction permits. Do not turn an estimated result period into a guaranteed deadline. Your own extra buffer belongs in the [B1 evidence timeline](/guides/germany-b1-settlement-citizenship-timeline/) as a planning choice.

### Original asset: centre enquiry script

> I am checking an adult Goethe-Zertifikat B1 booking at your centre. I intend to take [named modules / all required modules] on [proposed date] and hold [existing result documents, if relevant]. Please identify the current official booking page, fee and currency for this scope, participant-category conditions, registration and payment deadlines, and cancellation or rescheduling terms. Please also confirm the identification instructions, results process and certificate document offered. My receiving authority’s document requirement is [exact wording]; I will confirm acceptance with that authority separately.

Save the response and its date beside the official page. Remove placeholders before sending, and keep identity documents out of an initial fee enquiry.

### Scenario: two offers use different units

One page shows a module price and another advertises a complete booking. Mark them as different scopes in the worksheet. Ask each centre for the offer matching your intended modules and category, then compare the confirmed terms. Until both offers describe the same task, the record should say “not yet comparable”; it should not declare a cheaper provider.

### Original asset: booking four-party responsibility map

Assign each entry in your booking record to one of these parties. This prevents a payment confirmation from being mistaken for a document decision.

- **Receiving authority:** owns the acceptance question for your actual application. Record its instruction about the certificate documents, plus the route and date. Return here if the requested document form changes.
- **Goethe-Institut:** defines the examination product, module and certificate rules. Record the exact rule you need to understand.
- **Selected centre:** supplies the current offer, transaction terms and exam-day instructions. Record its official booking channel, contact and dated response. Ask it to resolve any discrepancy between the quote and checkout before paying.
- **You, the candidate:** choose your affordable budget, travel arrangements and personal buffer. Record these as your decisions, not official requirements. Keep receipts and confirmations together so you can explain what was actually booked if a later question arises.

When an answer is missing, the map gives you a specific next contact. For example, uncertainty about a category discount belongs with the centre; uncertainty about whether separate certificates fit an application belongs with the receiving authority.

### Scenario: you already hold some module certificates

You want a booking that completes your planned evidence rather than paying for a scope you have not checked. First list the actual module documents you hold and what you still intend to take. Use the current Goethe certificate rules linked from the [B1 comparison](/guides/goethe-b1-vs-telc-b1/) to frame your question, then ask the selected centre which booking and certificate document it can offer for that history.

Next, compare that proposed document with the receiving authority’s instruction. If the centre can offer module certificates but the authority’s wording is unclear, do not assume that paying for another module resolves the acceptance question. Save the centre’s answer and ask the authority about the proposed documents. The result may be a clear next booking, or it may remain an unresolved document question; the worksheet should show which one you actually have.

### Check the quote again at the transaction step

Before accepting the centre’s terms, compare the product, scope, category and amount shown at that moment with your saved quote. If any field differs, capture the current wording and seek clarification through the centre’s official contact. Ask the centre to confirm it against your saved quote.

After receiving a booking confirmation, write its reference and confirmed scope into your record. Keep any personal travel or application plans separate from what the centre has confirmed. If a later message changes an appointment or instruction, update the affected entry and identify the consequence for your personal plan; do not overwrite the earlier evidence without keeping its date.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Recording a price without its unit:** state whether it covers named modules or the intended complete booking.
- **Assuming a participant category applies:** ask what evidence the centre needs before using the discounted amount in your budget.
- **Equating a payment with a confirmed place:** retain the centre’s actual registration confirmation and check what it says.
- **Planning certificate delivery from task duration:** obtain the centre’s results and document process separately.
- **Reusing another centre’s conditions:** follow the institution handling your transaction, including its current cancellation and change instructions.
