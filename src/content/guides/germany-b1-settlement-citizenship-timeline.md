## Who this page is for

Use this page to put a settlement-permit file or a naturalisation file into a step order you can verify. The order comes from offices and exam rules you can open. A missing date stays blank. This timeline does not calculate residence periods, eligibility or processing time. It does not decide a settlement or citizenship outcome.

Confirm the route before you date anything. Settlement starts on [Germany settlement permit and B1](/guides/goethe-b1-germany-settlement-work/). Naturalisation starts on [citizenship language proof](/guides/germany-b1-citizenship-language-proof/). The [B1 hub](/germany-b1-settlement-citizenship/) keeps the files apart. Language proof and civic-knowledge proof are different items; the [Leben in Deutschland page](/guides/germany-b1-leben-in-deutschland-and-language-proof/) holds that split.

## Statute boundaries rechecked on 2026-11-10

The five-year section 10 (1) line, the limit that BGBl. 2025 I No. 256 deletes subsection 3 only, and the instruction not to choose the English three-year sentence are on the [B1 hub](/germany-b1-settlement-citizenship/). Language evidence and civic-knowledge evidence stay separate. Settlement under Residence Act (AufenthG) section 9 is a different procedure from naturalisation under the Nationality Act. This page does not decide either procedure and does not copy fees.

Those texts do not give this page a processing time. This timeline does not calculate the five-year condition or any other residence period. Do not copy the older “usually eight years / seven with the integration-course certificate” sentences from BAMF’s final-examination page.

The federal naturalisation portal describes a step order and also publishes fee and timing figures. Read any figure on the portal if you need the portal’s own wording, and ask the competent authority which statement applies to your file. This page does not repeat those figures.

If the BAMF-NAvI foreigners-authority locator does not open, use the contact route published by your town, district, or foreigners authority. The federal portal tells readers to ask the town, district, or foreigners authority which nationality authority is competent.

## The step order you can verify

Follow this order. A step without a saved source stays **unknown**.

1. **Authority.** Save who is competent, which route they named, and the date you checked. Settlement and naturalisation do not share one office by default.
2. **Two evidence lines.** Write the language document and the civic-knowledge document as separate lines. DTZ, Goethe B1, and telc B1 are not the same product, and none of them is the naturalisation test or Life in Germany. Compare them only after the authority has left a product open, in [DTZ, Goethe B1, and telc B1](/guides/goethe-b1-vs-telc-b1/).
3. **Centre, for an exam the authority named.** Record the exact product, the seat, registration, the ID the centre requires, the fee the centre states, cancellation, and the result and certificate process the centre confirms. Use only dates entered by you or confirmed by the competent authority and selected centre.
4. **Result and certificate.** Leave the issue date **unknown** unless that centre has confirmed its process. Goethe’s Exam Guidelines say the sitting centre generally notifies the result and that an early individual release is not possible. They do not state a national number of days from the exam to the certificate. telc’s regulations say evaluation starts when telc has the complete file, and that the centre hands the result notice to the candidate within two weeks of **receiving** it. That is not “the certificate arrives a set number of days after the exam”.
5. **Reader-set pause.** Add time only if you choose to, after you can see the confirmed dates. That pause is yours. It is not an official processing time.
6. **Submission recheck.** Open the authority’s current instructions again, then use the [submission checklist](/guides/germany-b1-settlement-citizenship-checklist/).

Do not copy another centre's result period, assume a retake seat, or create a standard appointment wait. Goethe’s Exam Guidelines allow resits and also allow the centre to set an interval; they give no right to a particular date. If translation or legalisation matters, use the authority’s instruction and a date the provider has confirmed.

Goethe’s “about five days” notice of the clock time, and the “usually within 14 days” sentence for non-modular oral and written parts, are rules in the Exam Guidelines. Goethe B1 is modular, so the 14-day sentence is not a description of every B1 module sitting. Neither sentence is a visa or naturalisation countdown. Confirm the sitting with the centre.

## FAQ

### How many days after the exam will the certificate be issued?

Unknown, on this page. The Goethe guidelines and the telc regulations describe who notifies you and when a centre must pass on a notice it has already received. They do not publish a national certificate-issue day. Ask the centre that booked you.

### How long will the authority take to decide?

This page does not state a decision-processing time. The federal portal publishes its own remark and says timing depends on the case. Ask the competent authority which statement applies, and read the portal rather than a copy of its number.

### Can I start from an exam date before the authority has named the proof?

No. An exam date before proof acceptance is a private hope. Mark the proof line unknown and resolve it with the authority first.

### What if BAMF-NAvI does not open?

Use the contact details your town, district, or foreigners authority publishes. The locator is a tool. It is not the only way to find the office, and it does not decide the file.

## Common mistakes

- Starting from an exam date before proof acceptance is clear.
- Treating a general result estimate as a certificate date.
- Assuming a failed attempt can be repeated on a date you have not been offered.
- Using this sequence as a residence-duration calculation.
- Copying the final-examination page’s outdated residence years.
- Treating Goethe’s B1 product HTML as opened. It was not reachable here and stays **needs-verify**.
