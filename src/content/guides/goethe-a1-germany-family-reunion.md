## Quick answer

The German mission that receives your visa file decides whether a Goethe certificate is recognised. The BAMF leaflet on basic German for spousal reunification, rechecked on 2026-11-11, names “Start Deutsch 1” of the Goethe-Institut among the ALTE-standard certificates it currently associates with that spouse situation. That name is not a booking order and not a decision for your file.

This page is the Goethe product path. Whether your file needs A1 at all stays on the [language-requirement guide](/guides/german-family-reunion-language-requirement/). Matching Goethe against telc, ÖSD, and TestDaF stays on [Goethe A1 vs telc A1](/guides/goethe-a1-vs-telc-a1/). This page does not decide a visa, an exemption, or an exam result.

The Federal Foreign Office spouse-language FAQ, opened 2026-11-11, states a general basic-knowledge rule and points you to BAMF and to the mission. It does not list certificates. Embassy pages for Dhaka, Yaoundé, Singapore, and Bern were opened the same day as samples of local instructions. They are not your list unless you apply at that mission.

## What the opened Goethe rules support

Checked on 2026-11-11 against the Goethe-Institut Exam Guidelines (Stand 1 September 2025) and the A1 Start Deutsch 1 administration rules of the same date. Those PDFs describe the exam. They are not an acceptance list.

The guidelines list **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** as a non-modular exam. A candidate who takes and passes all parts receives one certificate that shows the four parts. The administration rules describe those parts as listening, reading, writing, and speaking. You do not book them as separate modules.

The same guidelines say:

- The centre sets the registration deadline, place, and time. The fee follows the local fee schedule. There is no national fee on these PDFs, and this page does not invent one.
- The exact clock time is sent in text about five days before the exam date. That sentence is a notice of the sitting. It is not a buffer you can paste onto a visa countdown.
- For an exam that cannot be taken in modules, the oral and written parts are usually held within 14 days of each other. That is a window between parts, not a result date and not a certificate date.
- Results are generally notified by the centre where you sat the exam. An early individual release is not possible. The guidelines do not state a national number of days from the exam to the certificate.
- The whole exam may be repeated. The centre can set a period before a resit and tells you that period when it notifies the result. There is no right to a particular next date. Ask the centre. Do not turn a neighbour’s gap into your plan.

The certificate-check service at [goethe.de/verify](https://www.goethe.de/verify) was reachable on 2026-11-11. It checks whether a Goethe certificate is authentic. It does not accept the certificate for a visa and it does not show a seat.

## What stays needs-verify

Goethe product, overview, and centre HTML returned HTTP 403 on 2026-11-11. They stay **needs-verify**. Do not treat [the overview](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/prf.html) or [the A1 product page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/de/prf/prf/gzsd1.html) as pages this guide has read. The certificate name above comes from the BAMF leaflet and from the exam guidelines, not from those HTML pages.

These items are unknown until the office that owns them answers:

- Whether your mission accepts Goethe Start Deutsch 1 for your relationship and age category.
- Certificate age, originals, copies, or translations. The Federal Foreign Office certificate FAQ, Welche Sprachzertifikate werden im Visumsverfahren akzeptiert, read from the 2026-11-14 snapshot, says that as a rule (in der Regel) the exam date should not be more than one year old. If individual modules were repeated, the date of the oldest passed module applies. That “as a rule” guidance is not a statute that the certificate expires automatically, and the mission that receives the file can still give a case instruction. The shorter spouse-language FAQ does not state an age rule. Its silence does not cancel the certificate FAQ. The BAMF spousal leaflet (imprint 03/2023) also does not state a nationwide maximum age. Do not turn either silence into “the Foreign Office has no one-year guidance,” and do not turn the certificate FAQ into a hard nationwide expiry.
- How your mission runs the interview sentence in the leaflet: if the interview shows you undoubtedly have the basic command, no separate certificate is needed. That is not a waiver you can award yourself.
- The fee, the next seat, and the day the certificate will be in your hands.

## Product path, after the requirement is clear

1. If you still do not know whether A1 applies, return to the [requirement guide](/guides/german-family-reunion-language-requirement/). Do not open a booking form.
2. Quote the certificate words on your mission’s current page. If those words are not Goethe-Institut “Start Deutsch 1”, or the mission is silent, ask the mission. Use the [comparison](/guides/goethe-a1-vs-telc-a1/) only to line up names it has used.
3. If the mission has named this Goethe product, book only through a channel the exam owner or the mission identifies. Confirm the fee, the date, the exact identification document the test centre currently requires, cancellation, and how the certificate is issued. Write the centre’s figures in your notes. Do not copy them onto this page.
4. Do not convert the “about five days” notice or the “usually 14 days” window between parts into a visa appointment. Unknown certificate days stay unknown.
5. If the mission names more than one product, stop using brand familiarity. The comparison page is the next check.

## Common mistakes

- Booking Goethe because the leaflet names it, before your mission’s page does.
- Reading a Goethe product HTML page that did not open as if its marketing text had been checked.
- Treating a course letter, a youth exam, or a practice test as Start Deutsch 1.
- Treating telc “Start Deutsch 1” and “telc Deutsch A1 für Zuwanderer” as the same title. That split lives on the comparison page.
- Using the five-day notice or the 14-day window between parts as a national result day.
- Copying Dhaka, Yaoundé, Singapore, or Bern into a file for a different mission.
