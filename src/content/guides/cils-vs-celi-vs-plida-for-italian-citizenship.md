## Start after the requirement decision

Use this page only after your authority record identifies a citizenship procedure and gives you a reason to compare certificate options.

The current Foreign Ministry page lists the owners of CILS, CELI and PLIDA among the admitted certification bodies within its stated procedure. It also lists other bodies and qualifying evidence. This comparison is therefore not a complete list of every possible evidence path, and it does not turn one provider into the default choice.

The Italian Ministry of the Interior, competent Prefettura or responsible consulate remains the final decision source. CILS, CELI and PLIDA owners control their products; selected authorised centres control current local execution.

## Keep four decisions separate

| Decision | Source that controls it |
|---|---|
| Which citizenship basis applies | Italian citizenship authority, competent Prefettura or responsible consulate |
| Which evidence the procedure accepts | The current authority instruction or a traceable reply from the competent office |
| What each certificate product is | University for Foreigners of Siena for CILS; University for Foreigners of Perugia/CVCL for CELI; Società Dante Alighieri for PLIDA |
| Current centre, registration and local execution | The selected authorised centre, checked against the relevant product owner |

An exam owner's product page does not decide the first two rows. An authority page does not maintain every centre's current booking conditions.

## Compare the three products without ranking them

| Product | Product fact to verify | Local check before booking |
|---|---|---|
| CILS | The exact Siena certificate and whether the citizenship-labelled B1 product matches the authority record | Authorised centre, current session, registration terms, identity rules and result process |
| CELI | The exact Perugia/CVCL certificate, including whether the relevant option is CELI 2 or CELI 2 i cittadinanza | Availability at the selected centre, because session types and local terms can differ |
| PLIDA | The exact adult PLIDA B1 product and current owner rules | The selected PLIDA centre, current session and local registration conditions |

Do not rank the products by a generic claim such as “easiest,” “fastest” or “most accepted.” Compare only facts that matter to your saved authority instruction and the centre you can actually use.

## Build a certificate comparison record

For each candidate product, record:

1. The competent authority instruction and the date you checked it.
2. The exact certificate name and owner URL.
3. Why that product appears relevant to the authority wording.
4. The selected authorised centre and its current registration page.
5. The local conditions that still need confirmation.
6. Any unresolved acceptance, exemption or document-form question that must go back to the competent authority.

Keep product facts and authority decisions in separate columns. This prevents a changed exam calendar from being mistaken for a change in citizenship requirements.

## When to stop the comparison

Stop before booking if the authority record does not identify the relevant evidence, if the product name is ambiguous or if the selected centre cannot confirm the exact product and current terms. The responsible Prefettura, consulate or other competent authority—not the comparison table—must resolve an individual evidence question.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing providers before confirming the citizenship requirement.
- Assuming the three products are the only possible evidence paths.
- Treating a B1 label as proof that the exact certificate will satisfy an individual file.
- Comparing outdated prices or dates instead of current centre records.
- Asking an exam owner to decide an exemption or citizenship outcome.
- Treating a booking confirmation as proof of future acceptance.

## Next action

Return to the requirement page if you do not yet have a saved authority instruction. Otherwise, complete one comparison record for every product you can realistically take and remove any option whose exact product or authorised centre cannot be verified.

Before paying, send unresolved evidence questions to the competent Prefettura or responsible consulate and local execution questions to the selected authorised centre. This comparison is terminal; it does not create another content step.
