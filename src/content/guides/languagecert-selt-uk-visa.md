## Who this applies to

Use this page for a **provider-choice SELT product verification** after you have already confirmed that your UK route requires an approved English test. It is for readers comparing whether a LanguageCert SELT product matches the current Home Office list, the required CEFR level, the required skills, and the approved location rule.

This page is not a substitute for the route-first requirement check. If you have not yet confirmed whether your route needs SELT evidence, return to the IELTS UKVI guide or the relevant GOV.UK route page before comparing providers.

## Start with the Home Office list, not the provider brand

The Home Office or GOV.UK route requirement decides whether English evidence is needed and what type of evidence can be used. LanguageCert defines its exam product, booking process, identity rules, centre information, and candidate terms, but LanguageCert does not decide an individual visa, settlement, or citizenship outcome.

A provider being named on an approved list does not prove that every LanguageCert test, location, skill combination, or certificate use will match your route. Treat the GOV.UK list and the LanguageCert product page as two separate checks that must agree before you book.

## Check the exact LanguageCert product before booking

Use this checklist before paying or relying on a booking:

- Reopen the Home Office or GOV.UK instruction for your confirmed route.
- Record the required CEFR level, skills, approved provider wording, product name, and approved-location rule.
- Match that wording to the exact LanguageCert SELT product currently listed for UK use. The GOV.UK page read on 2026-11-14 says that from 6 July 2025 the LANGUAGECERT ESOL SELT 4-skills test was replaced by Academic SELT and General SELT. An older 4-skills result that remains valid is not the same fact as a new booking of the replaced test.
- Check the LanguageCert product page for candidate identity rules, booking terms, resit or cancellation terms, and local execution details.
- Save the route instruction, approved-list entry, and provider booking terms together so you can recheck them before submission.

## Why this provider page does not replace the Home Office record

This page checks one LanguageCert SELT product against the Home Office requirement. The IELTS page checks IELTS products. IELTS is not a required earlier step. Neither provider page replaces the GOV.UK record.

If the route requirement changes or is unclear, do not use this provider page instead of the deciding authority. If the route requirement is clear, compare the named LanguageCert product against that same requirement rather than assuming the brand alone is enough.

## Common mistakes

- Treating the LanguageCert brand as proof that any LanguageCert English test is acceptable.
- Checking a provider booking page without rechecking the current Home Office SELT list.
- Ignoring whether the route asks for a specific CEFR level, skill set, approved product, or approved location.
- Assuming a provider approval means a visa, settlement, or citizenship result is decided.
- Saving only the booking confirmation and not the authority requirement used to choose that booking.

## Next action

If your Home Office or GOV.UK route requirement is already confirmed, compare the exact LanguageCert SELT product and approved location against that requirement before booking. If the requirement is not confirmed, open the official GOV.UK SELT page before choosing a provider.
