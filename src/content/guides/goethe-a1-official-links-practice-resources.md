## Who this is for

Create a small, traceable collection of adult A1 resources instead of collecting unverified question banks. This page provides a working method, not an individual exam or application decision.

## Key decisions

Choose the exact product or application task first. Keep official requirements separate from the original practice or checking activities below.

## Start with one official resource

Use the adult section of the official training page linked below. Record its title, URL, date accessed and the skill you will practise. Keep the answer material separate until you have attempted the task so that recognition of an answer is not mistaken for understanding.

## Build a resource card

Give each resource four fields: owner, product/version, intended skill and next exercise. Remove duplicates. If a file has no clear owner or claims to contain leaked upcoming questions, do not use it as your format reference.

## Separate reference from extra practice

Keep official material as the format anchor. Label your own drills and a teacher’s supplementary work as practice, not official examination questions. Changing a name or date in an original exercise can test transfer without copying a protected sample into a public question bank.

## Keep an accessible alternative

If audio, PDF or browser controls are difficult to use, check the official digital training option and ask the provider about individual examination arrangements. A usable practice interface does not by itself confirm a test-day accommodation.

## Mistakes to avoid

Do not treat a sample, practice result or another person’s experience as confirmation of your own booking, certificate acceptance or readiness. Record unresolved questions rather than filling in dates, fees or outcomes.

## Next action

Complete the working record above, then use [the related guide](/guides/goethe-a1-30-day-study-plan/) to continue. Reopen the official source for any current product, local or application requirement.
