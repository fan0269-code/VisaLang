## Who this is for

Use this page only after you have identified **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** as an exact product to investigate and have a real location in mind. It gives four source-checked 2026 price records from two official Goethe scopes: the Goethe-Institut Germany price page and the named Goethe-Institut Beijing registration page. It is not a worldwide fee table.

The records help you understand which category question to ask and what evidence to save. The selected official or authorised centre remains the final source for its transaction.

## Key decisions

### Match the product before the price

The four rows apply to the adult **Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Start Deutsch 1** product. Do not attach them to Goethe-Zertifikat A1: Fit in Deutsch 1, a course, a mock examination, another level, or a service bundle. Save the exact product name from the official page and compare it with the selected booking channel.

### Match the source scope before the category

“Germany” below means the 2026 scope of the Goethe-Institut Germany price page. “Beijing” means the named Goethe-Institut Beijing page. Beijing is not a substitute for a China-wide price, and the Germany page does not establish the checkout of every independent exam partner. If your intended centre is outside those exact scopes, use the fallback rather than copying the nearest number.

### Treat category status as a question

The Germany page defines its own course-participant window. The Beijing page publishes a separate current-learner explanation and evidence instructions. Those source-controlled category definitions appear in the versioned table below only while their records remain current. Your booking channel must apply the category to the intended exam.

### Separate a price from a transaction

A published figure settles one thing only — the amount printed for that scope. Session, seat, registration, payment deadline, refund, result date, certificate delivery and mission acceptance each stay with their own owner. Before money moves, save the booking result and the current rules owned by the selected centre. A price-page screenshot alone is not a booking confirmation.

## Germany fee route

This guide publishes only the amounts the verified fee records above currently show for Germany; the steps below show how to confirm them at your own checkout.

Sources checked: 2026-09-09.

1. Open the [Goethe-Institut Germany 2026 examination fees](https://www.goethe.de/ins/de/en/prf/pre.html).
2. Find the adult Goethe-Zertifikat A1 (Start Deutsch 1 / SD1) row rather than the youth A1 row.
3. Read the course-participant remark and compare its definition with the intended examination date.
4. Continue to the selected official Goethe-Institut Germany booking route and confirm the product, candidate category, any tax or checkout detail, and transaction terms.
5. Save the page, booking evidence and checked date together.

This flow does not establish a price for every German exam partner. If the selected centre publishes a different current price or cannot apply the category, stop and ask that centre which terms govern its booking.

## Beijing fee route

This guide publishes only the amounts the verified fee records above currently show for Beijing; they cover the named Beijing institute only, not another Chinese city or partner.

Sources checked: 2026-09-09.

1. Open the [Goethe-Institut Beijing examination schedule and registration page](https://www.goethe.de/ins/cn/zh/spr/prf/anm.html).
2. Confirm that the price row is the adult A1/SD1 product and read the page's current-learner explanation.
3. Follow the current registration form and evidence instructions for the intended session.
4. Treat the registration result and official payment notice—not the price table—as evidence that the transaction may proceed.
5. Save the price row, category evidence request, registration result, payment terms and checked date.

This Beijing route does not prove another Chinese city or partner uses either amount or category. The page's current session list can also change; this guide deliberately does not copy a seat status.

## No currency conversion

Keep EUR and CNY as separate original-currency records. VisaLang does not apply an exchange rate, convert either amount, calculate a cross-currency total, or label one scope cheaper. Rates, card charges, bank fees and payment methods change independently and are not part of the four source records.

If you need a private household estimate, add your own dated bank or payment-provider assumption outside the official-fee column and label it as your assumption. Do not publish that conversion as a Goethe fee and do not use it to choose a centre before product, authority and local-execution checks are complete.

### Scenario: a Germany course may fit the source-defined window

The reader sees the source-controlled course-participant row. That is not yet an individual charge. The reader records the course end date, opens the intended booking route and asks which evidence it requires and whether the category applies to that examination date. Until the booking confirms it, the budget keeps the regular and conditional rows distinct rather than claiming a saving.

### Scenario: a Beijing learner has a course record

The reader sees the source-controlled Beijing current-learner row and has attended a Goethe course. The correct next step is not to select “current learner” by analogy. The reader follows the Beijing page's current definition and evidence instructions for the intended session, submits only through the official route, and waits for its registration and payment notice. A course document may support the question, but VisaLang cannot decide the category or promise a seat.

### Scenario: the booking page and saved price disagree

Pause rather than averaging the values or selecting the lower one. Save both pages with timestamps and use the selected centre's official contact route to ask which price, category and terms apply to the exact booking. An official page can describe a different scope or version, so pay once the centre's current answer covers your exact booking. Resume once that answer is attributable.

Store the exact product, official path to the selected centre, category definition, evidence request, fee and currency, payment recipient or channel, cancellation and rescheduling terms, result/certificate route, and checked date. ## Mistakes to avoid

- Copying a Beijing amount into a different Chinese city.
- Treating the Germany price page as every German partner's checkout.
- Claiming the conditional Germany row without the booking route applying the category.
- Assuming any Goethe course automatically creates Beijing current-learner status.
- Converting EUR and CNY to publish a “cheapest” centre.
- Treating a price, form or waiting-list entry as a seat.
- Mixing an exam fee with a course, travel, document or service bundle.
- Paying before saving the current cancellation, rescheduling and registration-result terms.
