## Start with a citizenship-basis requirement check

This page answers the question that comes before exam selection: **what current Italian-language evidence does the authority handling your citizenship application ask you to provide?**

The Ministry of the Interior application page currently identifies evidence of Italian knowledge at no lower than CEFR B1 and records exemptions within the procedure it describes. The Foreign Ministry citizenship page separately explains the evidence and competent consular process for applicants in its stated scope. These pages are the starting point, but the competent Prefettura or responsible consulate remains the final authority for the instruction applied to an individual file.

Do not begin by assuming that CILS is mandatory. The current authority pages also describe other certification bodies, qualifying study evidence and exemptions. Record which provision the competent authority says applies before paying for any exam.

## Build an authority record

Save a short requirement record before comparing certificates:

| Record | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Citizenship basis | The exact application route shown by the Ministry, competent Prefettura or responsible consulate |
| Receiving office | The office that will handle the file and the current official URL you checked |
| Language wording | The evidence wording for that route, without broadening it into a universal rule |
| Alternative evidence | Any qualifying study evidence or other route the authority page says may be relevant |
| Exemption question | The exact provision to ask the competent authority about; do not decide it yourself |
| Unresolved point | Any certificate name, document form or individual condition still requiring an official answer |

If this record does not establish why a certificate is needed, stop before choosing CILS, CELI or PLIDA.

## Separate the authority rule from the CILS product

The University for Foreigners of Siena owns the CILS product. Its current B1 page describes CILS UNO-B1 and identifies citizenship use within the product information. That source can establish what the CILS product is; it cannot decide which citizenship basis applies, whether another form of evidence is sufficient or whether an individual file satisfies the authority's instruction.

Only after the requirement record identifies CILS as a candidate should you use the CILS owner and selected authorised centre to verify the exact product, registration conditions, identity requirements and current local execution.

## Why the comparison page is a separate task

This is the **requirement page**. It establishes the authority instruction and the reason to investigate a certificate.

The [CILS, CELI and PLIDA comparison](/guides/cils-vs-celi-vs-plida-for-italian-citizenship/) starts later. It compares three named certificate products after the requirement is known. It does not replace this page because exam owners cannot decide the citizenship basis, an exemption or individual evidence acceptance.

## Common mistakes

- Starting from an exam calendar before confirming the citizenship basis.
- Treating the words “B1” and “CILS B1 Cittadinanza” as the same decision.
- Assuming CILS is the only evidence path because its product name mentions citizenship.
- Asking an exam centre to decide an exemption or an individual document question.
- Copying a fee, session, centre or result estimate from an undated third-party page.

## Next action

Open the current Ministry instruction for your citizenship basis and identify the competent Prefettura or responsible consulate. Save the language-evidence wording and mark each item as **confirmed by the authority**, **unresolved**, or **not applicable to this route**.

Continue to the certificate comparison only when that record shows why CILS, CELI, PLIDA or another admitted option should be investigated. If the instruction is unclear, ask the competent authority before booking.
