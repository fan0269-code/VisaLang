## Start with a programme-first requirement check

This page answers the question that comes before test selection: **what does IRCC currently require for the exact Express Entry programme you are using?**

IRCC's current language-test page covers the Federal Skilled Worker Program, Federal Skilled Trades Program and Canadian Experience Class. It lists TEF Canada and TCF Canada as accepted French tests within that Express Entry scope and publishes separate test-specific conversion information.

Do not broaden that finding to every Canadian immigration or citizenship procedure. Save the exact programme and current IRCC instruction before comparing exam products.

## Build an IRCC requirement record

Record the decision in this order:

| Record | What to capture |
|---|---|
| Express Entry programme | Federal Skilled Worker, Federal Skilled Trades or Canadian Experience Class |
| Language role | Whether French is being entered as the first or second official language for the profile |
| Accepted product | The exact current test name shown by IRCC, not a generic TEF or TCF label |
| Conversion source | The current IRCC table for that exact test and programme |
| Validity checkpoints | The IRCC result-validity instruction at profile creation and permanent-residence application |
| Unresolved point | Any profile, result-document or historical-score notice that still needs an IRCC answer |

If this record does not establish the programme and accepted product, stop before booking either test.

## Separate the IRCC rule from the TEF Canada product

IRCC controls Express Entry acceptance, programme language requirements, result-validity rules and the conversion used in the profile. Do not convert a TEF score from memory or with an unofficial table.

Le français des affaires, part of CCI Paris Île-de-France, owns TEF Canada product information. Its current candidate pages describe the four compulsory abilities and route candidates to official centres and registration conditions. The product owner and selected official centre can confirm the product and local execution; they do not decide Express Entry eligibility, points or application outcome.

## Why the comparison page is a separate task

This is the **programme-first requirement check**. It identifies the IRCC programme, accepted French-test options and official conversion path.

The [TEF Canada and TCF Canada comparison](/guides/tcf-canada-vs-tef/) starts later. It helps compare two product and centre workflows after the IRCC requirement record is complete. It does not replace the IRCC programme decision.

## Common mistakes

- Starting with the nearest exam centre before selecting the Express Entry programme.
- Treating a generic TEF or TCF test as the Canada-specific product named by IRCC.
- Entering a raw score without using IRCC's current test-specific table.
- Assuming an exam owner's immigration or citizenship description proves acceptance for another Canadian procedure.
- Copying a fee, date, seat or result estimate from a different centre or an undated page.
- Treating a calculated language level as a guarantee of points, an invitation or permanent residence.

## Next action

Open IRCC's current language-test page and complete one requirement record for the exact Express Entry programme. Save the accepted test names, the relevant test-specific table and the current validity instruction.

Continue to the comparison only when both TEF Canada and TCF Canada remain valid candidates for that recorded programme. Send profile or acceptance questions to IRCC; send product and local-execution questions to the selected official centre.
