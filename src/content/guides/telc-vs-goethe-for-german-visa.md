## Start with an acceptance record

This comparison starts only after the German mission, embassy, consulate or other competent receiving authority for your exact visa route has confirmed which language proof may be used. Save:

- the route and application location;
- the authority and official page or written instruction;
- the exact language level, certificate or result format named;
- the date you checked the instruction; and
- any unresolved question that must go back to that authority.

An exam owner can explain its own products. It cannot decide that a certificate will satisfy every German visa route, mission or applicant file.

## Compare exact products, not brands

"telc" and "Goethe" each cover more than one exam product. Compare only the exact products that remain after the authority check.

| Decision | Source that controls it | What to record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whether language proof applies to the visa route | The responsible German mission or competent receiving authority | Route, location, current instruction, level, proof format and open questions |
| Which exact exam products exist | Goethe-Institut for Goethe products; telc for telc products | Full product name, level, intended candidate group and current official product URL |
| Whether a result format is acceptable in the file | The responsible receiving authority | Full certificate or module/result requirements exactly as stated |
| Where and how the exam can be booked | The exam owner and selected official or authorised centre | Exact product, centre status, date, seat, fee, currency, ID, accessibility, cancellation, result and certificate process |

The Federal Foreign Office visa page is a process entry point, not a universal list of accepted certificates. Follow it to the mission responsible for your application and use that mission's current route instructions.

## Exam Overview

Goethe-Institut and telc each maintain multiple German exam products and levels. Use the exam owner's current page to identify the complete product name, level, candidate group, delivery format and component structure; a brand name or level alone is not a complete exam choice.

## Eligibility

The responsible German mission or competent receiving authority decides whether language proof applies to the exact route and which evidence may be used. The exam owner decides who may register for its product under its rules. Keep those two eligibility questions separate.

## Fees

Do not transfer a fee from another centre, country, candidate group or date. Obtain the current total price, currency, included services and payment terms from the selected official or authorised centre before paying.

## Dates

Use only a currently bookable date supplied by the selected centre for the exact product. A provider catalogue or centre finder does not prove a seat, registration deadline, result date or certificate-delivery date.

## Test Format

Read the current exam-owner product page and regulations for the exact product. Do not assume that two exams at the same CEFR level have the same components, module rules, delivery format or result document.

## Locations / Online

Verify the centre through Goethe-Institut or telc, then confirm that it currently offers the exact product. Do not interpret an online information page, digital exam label or preparation course as a remotely invigilated certificate exam.

## Required Documents

Maintain separate lists for registration, test day and the visa file. The selected centre controls its registration and test-day identification instructions; the responsible authority controls the documents and proof format submitted with the application.

## Passing Score

Use the exam owner's current regulations to understand how the exact product reports a result. Ask the receiving authority whether it requires a full certificate, particular modules or another result format; a provider pass result does not by itself establish visa acceptance.

## Retake Policy

Confirm repeat and rebooking rules with the exam owner and selected centre. Separately check any visa or submission timing with the responsible authority; neither VisaLang nor the centre can guarantee that another sitting will fit an application timeline.

## Prep Path

Prepare only after the route, exact product and current format are confirmed. Base the plan on the exam owner's current syllabus, regulations and official training material, then adapt the schedule to locally confirmed dates.

## Best Courses

This guide does not rank courses. If using a course, verify that it prepares for the exact current product and format; a course provider cannot confirm certificate acceptance or guarantee an exam result.

## Practice Test

Start with current official practice material linked by Goethe-Institut or telc for the exact product. Do not treat third-party questions, a practice score or a course-completion record as accepted visa proof.

## Career Outcome

An exam certificate does not guarantee a job, professional recognition, a licence, a visa or another administrative outcome. For a regulated profession, obtain the exact language-proof instruction from the competent recognition or licensing authority for the profession and place.

## Related Exams

Consider another exam only if the same receiving authority confirms that its exact product and result format remain candidate proof for the same route. A same-level label or another guide's example is not enough.

## Build a product comparison record

Create one row for each exact candidate product and record:

1. The full product name and level from the exam owner.
2. The authority instruction that keeps it in scope for your route.
3. Whether the authority requires a full certificate, particular modules or another result format.
4. The selected official or authorised centre and the page that confirms the exact product.
5. Current local terms that still need confirmation before payment.
6. A stop reason if the authority, product or centre information does not match.

Do not rank products by unverified difficulty, recognition, speed, pass rate or price. A nearby centre or provider claim is not a substitute for the receiving authority's instruction.

## When to stop

Pause the comparison if you do not know the exact visa route, application location, required level or accepted proof. Also stop if a centre advertises only a general level, cannot confirm the exact product, or cannot show that it is an official or authorised centre for that product.

Go back to the responsible authority for acceptance questions. Go to Goethe-Institut or telc for product questions, and to the selected centre for current local execution.

## Common mistakes

- Assuming every A1, B1 or B2 certificate is interchangeable.
- Treating an exam owner's recognition language as an individual visa decision.
- Comparing a full exam with a module or a different candidate-group product.
- Copying a fee, date, seat or result estimate from another country or centre.
- Booking before the authority record and exact product name agree.

## FAQ

### Does VisaLang decide whether Goethe or telc is accepted?

No. The responsible German mission or competent receiving authority decides which proof applies to the individual route and file.

### Can I compare fees or dates before the authority check?

You may collect local options, but do not pay until the authority instruction, exact product and selected official or authorised centre agree.

### Does the same CEFR level make two certificates interchangeable?

No. Compare complete product names, modules or components, result documents and the receiving authority's current wording.
