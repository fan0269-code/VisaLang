## Who this is for

Use this as a final evidence-recheck aid after you have identified a German settlement-permit or naturalisation route. It is for a reader comparing their own record against the competent authority’s current instruction. The word “B1” should never make the wider application invisible.

The national law identifies parts of a general framework; the competent local foreigners or nationality authority assesses the particular route and complete application. An exam owner defines its product and a selected centre handles its local administration. Keep those roles separate in the checklist. A product certificate may be an item to ask about; the competent authority decides whether it answers the file's evidence question.

## Key decisions

First decide which route and authority own the file. For the general settlement permit, Section 9 of the Residence Act is a national-law reference; for general naturalisation, Section 10 of the Nationality Act is a national-law reference.

Second decide how to separate evidence. Keep a language-evidence row, a civic-knowledge row where relevant, and a wider-file row. The wider row may contain identity, residence history, livelihood, housing, insurance, declarations, forms or other conditions, but only the authority’s current route-specific instruction tells you which ones matter. Do not make a negative inference from an item this planning page does not mention.

Third decide whether each line is confirmed, not applicable according to the authority, or unresolved. “Not applicable” needs an authority source and date; it is not a personal assumption. If the document is an exam result, also record the owner’s exact product name and the centre’s local evidence, but do not let that evidence answer the authority-acceptance field unless the authority has confirmed it.

### Scenario: the naturalisation checklist mentions two evidence categories

Open separate rows for language and civic knowledge. Ask the authority which documents and formats it accepts for both, then attach the dated response to each row. Keep other statutory and local items elsewhere. A passed exam may fill one confirmed record, but it does not mark the entire application complete.

### Scenario: a settlement applicant has a certificate but an old local PDF

Retain the certificate and old PDF, but fetch the current authority instruction or request a written clarification. Mark the proof row “published but needs local confirmation” until the authority answers. Do not assume that the old local procedure, a neighbouring authority’s checklist or a portal overview still applies.

### Original asset: checklist four-owner evidence map and submission board

Make a checklist four-owner evidence map for national law, competent local authority, exam owner and selected centre. Use lanes named Route and procedure, Evidence, and Wider file. Each card needs: requirement wording, deciding owner, official source or reply, checked date, document held, format instruction, status and next verification action. This makes it clear that a language certificate is one evidence card rather than an approval card.

### Original asset: pre-submission mismatch log

For every mismatch, write the two sources, who owns the answer, the question sent, date sent, response and resolution. Examples include a general portal versus a local form, a centre message versus a product page, or a certificate title versus the authority’s wording. Wait for the owner of the disputed claim to explain which instruction applies.

### Original asset: final five-question gate

Ask: Is the route identified? Is the competent authority and current submission channel confirmed? Is each language or civic document accepted in the required format? Are all non-language categories compared to the current instruction? Does every unresolved item have an authority-owned next action? A “no” is a recheck instruction, not proof that the application fails.

## A recheck, not a universal document list

Use this as a recheck against the authority's current instruction: it establishes no eligibility, exception, proof acceptance or complete file, and the settlement and naturalisation routes stay separate.

## Choose an independent checklist

Follow the provision your authority has identified: [general settlement](#settlement-checklist), [skilled-worker settlement](#skilled-settlement-checklist), [Blue Card settlement](#blue-card-checklist), or [citizenship](#citizenship-checklist). If it is not known or is absent here, use the [unresolved-route checklist](#unknown-route-checklist). Keep only the checklist for the procedure you are working on; none marks an application ready or eligible.

- Record the § 18c(1) service and ask whether it applies to your current title: ___.
- **Language:** requested proof and document format ___; authority answer ___.
- **Knowledge:** separate § 9(2), number 8 evidence instruction ___; document ___.
- **Wider file:** ask the authority which qualifying-title, employment and contribution records, and remaining cross-referenced evidence, to provide: ___.
- If German vocational training or study is relevant, request the authority's assessment of that branch rather than calculating a personal period here: ___.

**Skilled-worker next action:** obtain the foreigners authority's § 18c(1) checklist and resolve the employment/contribution-record question before filling dates in the [evidence timeline](/guides/germany-b1-settlement-citizenship-timeline/). This is settlement preparation; it creates no general B1 work-visa rule.

<h3 id="blue-card-checklist">EU Blue Card settlement checklist — § 18c(2) AufenthG</h3>

- Record the § 18c(2) service and responsible foreigners authority: ___.
- **Language branch:** authority-confirmed A1 or B1 branch ___; requested evidence ___; format ___.
- **Knowledge:** separate knowledge-evidence request ___; result or alternative document ___.
- **Wider file:** authority request for qualifying employment, contributions and other cross-referenced conditions ___; records still missing ___.
- **Dates:** store the authority's response about qualifying periods separately from the exam-centre calendar. Do not turn an exam pass date into an application date.

**Blue Card next action:** ask the foreigners authority to resolve the language branch and employment-record question together. An unexplained “B1” on a generic checklist is a reason to ask, not a reason to assume that only B1 can fit § 18c(2).

<h3 id="unknown-route-checklist">Unresolved-route checklist</h3>

- Procedure you intend to ask about ___; provision on the authority page or residence title ___; if absent, write “unknown”.
- Official city or district website ___; foreigners authority for settlement or nationality authority for naturalisation ___; confirmed contact channel ___.
- Ask: “Which statutory route and current service apply, and which language, knowledge and wider-file documents should I prepare?”
- Leave all three evidence lanes unresolved. Do not assign a language level, test purchase, qualifying date or eligibility result.

**Unknown-route next action:** obtain the authority's route-specific instruction. If its provision is outside these checklists, continue with that official instruction instead of forcing it into a listed branch.

The route references are [§ 9](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__9.html), [§ 18c](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__18c.html), [§ 2(9), (11)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/aufenthg_2004/__2.html) and [StAG § 10–11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stag/BJNR005830913.html), checked 13 September 2026. These worksheets organise questions; the competent authority assesses the actual requirements and evidence.

## Route and authority

- Record the exact application route or statutory provision shown by the authority.
- Confirm the competent authority, location, current procedure and submission channel.
- Save the current page, portal, form, PDF or written reply and date checked.
- Resolve any conflict between a general overview and the local instruction.

## Language and civic-knowledge evidence

- Record the exact language level, accepted document and required format.
- Keep the exam owner's product information separate from the authority's acceptance decision.
- For naturalisation or another relevant route, ask whether civic-knowledge evidence is separately required and which proof is accepted.
- Recheck centre-specific dates, identification, fee, cancellation, result and certificate details before relying on them.
- File the document you hold in the right lane with the [Leben in Deutschland and DTZ lanes](/guides/germany-b1-leben-in-deutschland-and-language-proof/) before asking the authority to confirm it.

## Wider application file

Use the authority's own checklist for identity, residence history, livelihood, housing, insurance, declarations, forms and any other conditions.

For each item, record `confirmed`, `not applicable according to the authority`, or `unresolved`. Leave unresolved as written.

## Working sequence

Read the authority’s current instructions first. Populate only facts that an appropriate owner supports. Cross-reference national law for the general route, product information for exam facts and the selected centre for local administration. Review all entries shortly before submission because an old note or downloaded form may no longer be current.

## Checklist handoff

Keep the completed board as a snapshot, not a substitute for a current local instruction. A later authority reply can change one card without deciding every other card.

## Mistakes to avoid

- Treating B1 as the entire settlement or naturalisation file.
- Merging language evidence and civic-knowledge evidence.
- Reusing an old form or another authority's checklist.
- Submitting before resolving a mismatch in document format or procedure.

## Next action

This is the terminal review step. Where the gap concerns the route, return to the [settlement route guide](/guides/goethe-b1-germany-settlement-work/) or [citizenship language-proof guide](/guides/germany-b1-citizenship-language-proof/). Where the gap is only an exam-centre date or term, verify directly with the selected centre rather than reopening the legal-route sequence. Recheck before submitting whenever a document has changed or an instruction is older than your record.
