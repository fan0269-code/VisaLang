## Who this plan is for

Use this after a settlement or citizenship file has a language question and Goethe-Zertifikat B1 is one product you may need. It is the only B1 study plan. The retired difficulty page’s module self-check now lives here, without any claim about how often candidates fail. Current adult sample papers were not reopened. Format facts are from the B1 administration rules, Stand 1 September 2025.

## Module boundary

The rules say the exam for adults and young learners has four modules that can be taken separately or together: reading (parts 1–5), listening (parts 1–4), writing (parts 1–3), and speaking (parts 1–3). If all four are taken on one date, the written modules generally come first and last about 165 minutes with no breaks: reading 65, listening about 40, writing 60. Speaking is generally in pairs for about 15 minutes, or about 10 minutes alone, after 15 minutes of preparation. Each module is scored out of 100. A module is passed at 60 points. Ratings in section 6.2 run from very good (100–90) down to fail (59–0). Do not average modules into an admissions result, and do not treat a module pass as acceptance by a foreigners authority or a nationality authority.

## Self-check before the eight weeks

Answer from your own last attempt or from an original drill. “Possible” means a reason to change this plan. It is not a frequency.

| Module | What you can observe | If you see it, change the plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reading | You chose an option because one word matched, and you cannot say why the other options fail | Spend the next block only on rejecting options, using a text you wrote or a public notice |
| Listening | A number, date, or attitude was missed after the audio moved on | Replay once for that category only. Do not add a new topic list |
| Writing | One prompt point is missing, or the reader of the message is unclear | Number the prompt points before the next draft |
| Speaking | The partner’s question was not answered, or a misunderstanding was not repaired | Repeat one turn that includes a repair phrase in German |

## Eight-week view

Weeks 1–2: one untimed pass of each module’s shape, using original prompts. Record the self-check above. Do not call an unread sample “already checked”.

Weeks 3–6: two modules per week, the ones the self-check marked. The other two get one short maintenance block. Skill pages ([listening](/guides/goethe-b1-listening-deep-dive/), [speaking](/guides/goethe-b1-speaking-topics/), [writing](/guides/goethe-b1-writing-assessment/), [mock routine](/guides/goethe-b1-mock-exam-routine/)) are the drills. They do not each carry another four-week calendar.

Weeks 7–8: one timed run of the modules you will actually book. If the centre offers single modules, book only the module the authority’s wording still requires. If the sitting is the whole exam, section 15 of the Exam Guidelines is about resits of the examinations it names; confirm the B1 resit rule with the centre because modular exams can repeat a module. Do not invent the interval.

Stop the calendar if the authority has not named the document, or if you cannot state which module failed your own check.

## Common mistakes

- Saying a module is “usually” the one people fail.
- Copying a points table from an adult training booklet this review did not open.
- Using the plan to decide section 9, section 18c, or section 10.
